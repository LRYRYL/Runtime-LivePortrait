Save your portrait here as  my_face.jpg  (or my_face.png).
Scripts 1 and 3 will pick it up automatically.

Choosing a good photo:
  * frontal face, looking at the camera
  * whole head visible (do not crop the chin)
  * face fills a good part of the frame
  * even lighting, no heavy shadows
  * 512px or larger on the short side

Avoid: profile views, looking down, sunglasses, very small faces.
